# D3 Coverage Stratification Audit

This zero-training audit reaggregates the accepted D3 empirical-interval membership indicators. It neither fits a model nor changes interval widths.

The primary denominator contains 34,475 record-by-seed slots: 6,895 curated records evaluated under five algorithmic seeds. Those seed realizations are repeated computational predictions, not independent experimental observations, so no binomial confidence interval is reported.

At nominal 90% coverage, slot-pooled coverage was 77.0%, the five seed estimates ranged from 75.8% to 78.1%, equal-source macro coverage was 71.4%, and equal-block macro coverage was 75.0%. Corresponding slot-pooled/equal-source/equal-block values were 44.1%/31.9%/28.4% at nominal 50% and 67.2%/58.6%/59.5% at nominal 80%. All higher-level aggregations therefore preserve the conclusion of under-coverage.

Block-level coverage ranged from 0 to 1 at the two higher nominal levels. This range is descriptive and reflects both regime heterogeneity and strongly unequal block sizes; it is not evidence for 18 equally precise calibration estimates.
